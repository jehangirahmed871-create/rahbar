# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2027 edition)

This edition replaces the 2026 edition from the Fall 2027 semester.

## 1. Attendance

Students must attend at least 82 percent of the lectures of each course to be eligible for the final examination. Documented medical absence requests are reviewed by the Head of Department.

## 2. Academic warning

A student whose CGPA falls below 2.10 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic warning.

## 3. Repeating a course

Only the grade from the most recent attempt is included when calculating the cumulative GPA.
